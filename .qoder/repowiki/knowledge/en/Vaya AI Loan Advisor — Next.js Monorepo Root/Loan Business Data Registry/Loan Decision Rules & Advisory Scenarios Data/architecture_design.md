Three flat data files under `src/data/` that act as the single source of truth for business rules consumed by the decision engine. `eligibilityRules.ts` exports an array of `EligibilityRule` objects keyed by `package_id` (e.g. `vcb-oto-01`, `bidv-nha-01`) with Vietnamese field names (`dieu_kien`, `thu_nhap_toi_thieu`, `dti_toi_da`, `do_tuoi_min/max`). `riskRules.ts` defines its own `RiskRule` interface locally and exports `RISK_RULES` categorized by `DTI | DATA | TERM | AGE | INCOME` with `WARNING`/`REJECT` severity levels. `scenarios.ts` imports `AdvisoryScenario` from `@/lib/engine/scenarioMatcher` and exports `ADVISORY_SCENARIOS` describing customer segments with amount/income ranges and recommended terms. Dependency direction is one-way: these files depend on type definitions in `@/lib/engine/*` but expose no functions, keeping them pure data registries.
